Speech & language neuroscience

Zihua (James) Chen

PhD candidate in Neural Science · NYU · Long Lab

I use intracranial EEG (iEEG) to study how the human brain understands language and plans speech. I’m especially interested in how we prepare what to say next in conversation, and in using representations from language and speech models to test what neural activity encodes.
